# TreeShop products and service packages

Status: proposed offers for founder testing, September 2026. Prices are starting quotes, not published commitments. Reprice after tracking actual delivery time and client outcomes for the first two installations.

## What the customer buys

TreeShop provides an installed operating system plus an agreed level of ongoing work. Every tier receives a named operator, a defined reporting cadence, an open issue list, and a way to see what was done. Buckets is included as part of delivery; the customer does not have to buy separate software just to receive the service.

| Package | Proposed monthly fee | Included operating scope | Typical cadence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1. Buckets + Advisory | $2,500 | Buckets setup and maintenance, cost and pricing review, baseline scorecard, priorities and recommendations | Monthly owner review; limited implementation queue |
| 2. Digital Foundation | $4,500–$6,000 | Package 1 plus a custom company website, hosting coordination, technical upkeep, search visibility work including SEO and answer-oriented content | Biweekly operating review; monthly website and lead report |
| 3. Growth Operations | $6,500–$9,000 | Package 2 plus campaign planning, approved ad management, lead-source and quote-conversion review, marketing calendar, and sales process improvements | Weekly operating review; active campaign and sales queue |
| 4. Remote Leadership | $10,000–$15,000+ | Package 3 plus broader operating plan, budgets, cross-function priorities, manager coordination, and frequent execution review under a written authority schedule | Weekly leadership meeting and agreed working-day response rhythm |

The package number defines the recurring workload and decision responsibility. A website build, major rebrand, data migration, or custom software integration is scoped as a launch project with a price and acceptance criteria. Packages 2–4 include ongoing website work after that launch project.

## Launch fees and third-party costs

Proposed installation fees: Package 1, $3,000–$5,000; Package 2, $6,000–$12,000; Package 3, $8,000–$15,000; Package 4, quoted after discovery. For the first client, an existing relationship may justify a separate pilot agreement and a documented internal subsidy. Do not let pilot economics become the standard package by accident.

Client-paid costs sit outside the retainer: MacBook, domains, Google Workspace, Jobber, AI subscriptions, hosting, ad spend, media production, and outside specialists. TreeShop may procure or manage them if the client approves the vendor, budget, and account ownership.

## Scope controls that protect delivery quality

- Each package has a monthly work queue. At the start of each cycle, the client and TreeShop agree on the highest-value deliverables that fit the tier.
- A material new initiative receives a brief scope, price if additional, owner, and expected result before work begins.
- Ad spend is authorized separately from the management fee. No campaign goes live without an approved budget and owner.
- Reporting describes observed results and assumptions. TreeShop does not promise a specific revenue, margin, or search ranking outcome.
- The client can end the recurring engagement under the agreed notice period. A transition deliverable lists accounts, exports, open work, and access removal.

## Sales test

A buyer should be able to answer three questions after a TreeShop proposal: **What will TreeShop take responsibility for? What will I see each month? What do I still pay for separately?** If a package cannot answer those questions, tighten it before selling it.

The first case study should show a dated baseline, work performed, and measured changes in pricing accuracy, quote conversion, lead handling, schedule use, and cash collection where reliable data exists. Avoid attributing every business change to TreeShop without evidence.
